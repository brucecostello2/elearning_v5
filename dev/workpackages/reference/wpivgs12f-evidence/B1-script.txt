# Subtracting with Regrouping

Hi! Today we are going to learn how to subtract two-digit numbers when the top digit is too small.

Let's look at our problem: **52 minus 27.**

Write 52 on top and 27 underneath. The ones line up under the ones, and the tens line up under the tens.

## Step 1: Look at the ones column

In the ones column we have 2 minus 7.

But 2 is smaller than 7. We cannot take 7 away from 2 and stay in whole numbers.

So we need more ones. We go next door and borrow.

## Step 2: Regroup

Look at the tens column. There is a 5 there, which means 5 tens, or 50.

We take one of those tens and carry it over to the ones.

Cross out the 5 and write 4 above it. Now the tens column has 4 tens left.

That ten we borrowed becomes ten ones. Add it to the 2 that was already there.

2 plus 10 is 12. Write a small 1 in front of the 2 so the ones column now reads 12.

## Step 3: Subtract the ones

Now we can do it. 12 minus 7 is 5.

Write 5 underneath the ones column.

## Step 4: Subtract the tens

The tens column now reads 4 minus 2.

4 minus 2 is 2. Write 2 underneath the tens column.

## The answer

Reading our answer across, 52 minus 27 is **25**.

## Now you try

Here is one for you on your own.

**Work out 63 minus 48.**

Pause here. Do not read on yet. Set it up in columns, regroup if you need to, and find the answer yourself.

Take your time. Nobody is watching the clock.

When you have your answer, carry on.

Ready?

The answer is **15**.

If you got 15, brilliant. If you did not, check whether you remembered to cross out the 6 and make it a 5 when you borrowed. That is the step people forget.

## Remember

Borrow only when the top digit is smaller than the bottom one. Cross out the ten you took, and never forget to reduce the tens column by one.
